VELORA Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I place an order?

Choose your favourite product, add it to your cart, enter your delivery details and continue to payment.

2. How can I apply a coupon?

Enter VELORA10 or WELCOME20 in the coupon box during checkout.

3. What payment methods are available?

Our demo checkout offers Cash on Delivery, UPI Demo and Card Demo options.

4. How can I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter your order ID to view the demo order status.

5. How can I contact VELORA?

Visit our Contact Us page and fill in the contact form.

6. Can I view my previous orders?

Yes. Open My Orders to view your saved demo orders.
